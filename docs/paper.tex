\begin{abstract}
Autonomous bio-hybrid systems operating in communication-denied environments require sub-millisecond threat reflexes, metabolic homeostasis, and structural fault recovery without cloud reliance. We present BioEdge-SNN-Apex, a six-pillar edge-native control framework integrating a Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) Spiking Neural Network with Variational Free-Energy homeostasis and topological pathway self-repair. Benchmarked natively on ARM64 hardware across 1,000 stochastic trauma cycles, the system achieves 100.00\% survival with 363.13~$\mu$s mean edge latency, outperforming ablated baselines lacking self-repair (4.60\%), energy harvesting (9.70\%), or reflex arcs (38.30\%).
\end{abstract}
