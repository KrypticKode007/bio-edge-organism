\begin{abstract}
Autonomous bio-hybrid systems operating in communication-denied environments require sub-millisecond threat reflexes, metabolic homeostasis, and structural fault recovery without cloud reliance. We present BioEdge-SNN-Apex, a six-pillar edge-native control framework integrating a Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) Spiking Neural Network with Variational Free-Energy homeostasis, topological pathway self-repair, and decentralized peer-to-peer swarm coordination. Benchmarked natively on ARM64 hardware, the Python runtime achieves 100.00\% survival across 1,000 stochastic trauma cycles at 363.13~$\mu$s mean latency, while our zero-allocation C++11 microcontroller port executes complete inference and self-repair cycles in \textbf{90.97~ns} (\textbf{10.99~MHz}).
\end{abstract}
